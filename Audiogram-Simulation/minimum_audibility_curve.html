Select or drag a point to inspect its threshold. Standard axis: higher dB HL values appear higher.

Drag a dot vertically (or use arrow keys) to change its threshold by 10 dB. Begin by presenting a 1,000 Hz tone at 30 dB HL to familiarize yourself with the task. If you cannot hear the tone, increase the level to 50 dB HL and continue increasing it in 10 dB steps until you hear it. Then determine your initial hearing threshold. For each subsequent frequency (2,000, 4,000, 8,000, 500, 250, and 125 Hz), begin testing 10 dB above the threshold determined at the previously tested frequency. Once you hear the tone, decrease its intensity in 10 dB steps after each response until you can no longer hear it. Repeat this procedure until you determine the lowest intensity level at which you can hear the tone in at least two out of three ascending presentations. The green region is above threshold; peach is below threshold. Audio volume is illustrative, not calibrated to dB HL; start with your device volume low. This is not a hearing test.
